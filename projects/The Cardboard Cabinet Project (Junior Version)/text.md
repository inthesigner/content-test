# The Cardboard Cabinet Project (Junior Version)
The Junior Version explores the idea of turning a handheld gaming device into a compact arcade cabinet. Built primarily from cardboard, the structure is designed to house a Game Boy-style handheld device running Linux, which serves as the main computing unit as well as the gaming interface.

Rather than using traditional arcade controls, this version preserves the original handheld layout, with its integrated directional pad and action buttons. The device can therefore be used almost as it is, while the cardboard enclosure transforms the experience into something closer to a miniature arcade machine.

The project focuses on making the most of existing hardware, combining a lightweight and accessible construction with a reusable computing platform. The Linux installation offers the possibility of experimenting with different software environments, emulators and gaming applications, turning a familiar handheld device into the heart of a small custom-built arcade system.

The Junior Version is intended as a relatively simple and compact starting point for exploring the relationship between hardware reuse, digital fabrication and retro gaming.
